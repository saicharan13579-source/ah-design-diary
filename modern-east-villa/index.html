What the prices include

  • Supply and execution of every BOQ item at the quantities shown
  • Soil preparation: red earth, manure and compost mixes
  • Drip irrigation and landscape lighting as lump-sum allowances
  • GST (can be added in the budget panel)
  • Transportation
  • Site variation of 10–15% (can be added in the budget panel)
